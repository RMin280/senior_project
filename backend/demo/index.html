How the Clean Air Score works

Each day from 2021 to 2025 earns points by its official EPA AQI category: Good = 100, Moderate = 60, Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups = 20, anything worse = 0. The score is the average day. Grades: A 93+, B 85+, C 77+, D 70+, F below 70. Counties whose monitors don't measure ozone or fine particles (the two pollutants behind most bad-air days in Texas) are listed as limited data and not ranked.
